# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

This handbook describes how we work at Acme Robotics, a fictional company used for demos. It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors should refer to their contract.

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can organise your day freely, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

The standard working week is 40 hours. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager and is compensated with time off at a rate of 1:1.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day so people can meet in person.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year. You must notify HR at least two weeks before travelling, because longer stays can create tax obligations.

### Home Office Equipment

Every employee receives a one-time home office budget of 600 EUR for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment. Equipment bought with the budget belongs to the employee after 24 months.

## Leave

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a proportional amount based on their contracted hours.

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines requests within five working days.

#### Carry-over

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31; after that date they expire. Days beyond the 5-day limit expire on December 31.

### Sick Leave

If you are ill, inform your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of sick leave.

Sick days do not count against your vacation balance, including sick days that occur during a vacation if you provide a doctor's certificate.

### Parental Leave

Employees who become parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 18 months after the birth or adoption. Notify HR at least eight weeks before the first block starts.

## Onboarding

New employees are assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first three months. The buddy answers informal questions and introduces the new colleague to people across the company.

During the first week, every new employee completes the security awareness training described in the IT Security Policy. Access to production systems is granted only after this training is finished.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, conferences, books and certifications. Unused budget does not carry over into the next year.

Employees can also spend up to 5 working days per year on learning activities during working hours. Requests go through your manager.
